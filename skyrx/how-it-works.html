FROM CASE TO CONFIRMATION

Follow the response.

Three moments. A person accountable at every step.

01

PREPARE & DEPLOY

Ready at the station.

A proposed protective case keeps the aircraft at its starting point. The case opens and the drone lifts out after an operator has reviewed the exercise.

  • Review the request and select a modeled payload.
  • Check the aircraft, battery reserve, and candidate site.
  • Approve the simulated launch.

The simulator models this approval workflow. The physical case and aircraft shown here are concept artwork.

02

ARRIVE & CONFIRM

Arrival is only one step.

The proposed delivery goes to a site selected for the exercise. A responder confirms receipt before the software records the handoff.

  • Inspect the proposed route and transfer distance.
  • Hold or replan if the candidate site is blocked.
  • Record a human confirmation of the payload receipt.

Map markers are candidate locations, not approved landing zones. Real operations would need site assessment and authorization.

03

RECOVER & LEARN

Close the loop.

The aircraft must be accounted for, whether it returns to its modeled station or a responder confirms recovery. The event record preserves what happened.

  • Confirm recovery when an exercise requires it.
  • Keep a completed handoff recorded after a later disruption.
  • Replay the decisions or export the mission log.

Lost-link and battery scenarios are training behaviors, not certified aircraft failsafes.
