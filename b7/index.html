01 / Digital gold

Timeless by nature.
Digital by design.

One token.
One gram of gold.

The proposed model connects digital ownership to allocated gold and physical redemption.

Concept only. No live gold reserves. Ownership requires custody agreements; gold prices can fall.

How B7 would work

One B7 would represent one gram of fine gold. Supply would grow only after gold purchases are confirmed and allocated. There is no need to issue a large supply upfront.

Customers could request a supported cash payout or eligible physical-gold delivery under the proposed service. They would not have to swap B7 into BNB. Quotes, fees, minimums and availability would be disclosed.

BNB pays network fees; it is not the proposed gold backing. A network outage could still delay transfers and redemption. Real custody, payout and recovery services remain to be built.

Swiss custody, explained

The intended custody and gold-ownership agreements would be governed by Swiss law, with the metal stored in Switzerland. Those arrangements have not been established.

A real launch would need defined ownership rights, an appointed custodian, reserve verification, delivery terms, and protections if an operator fails. Swiss storage would not make B7 a Swiss bank or remove obligations elsewhere.

The prototype's purchases, reserves, and deliveries are simulated. Neither the demo nor its testnet token conveys ownership of physical gold.

THE ENGINEERING BEHIND B7

The idea.
Engineered.

Solidity, reserve accounting, and product design. A prototype by Joseph Bolkovatz.

01

The contract

Reserve-limited issuance. Explicit permissions.

confirmedReserve ≥ tokenSupply
Contract details

An ERC-20 prototype for BNB Smart Chain. Six decimal places represent fractions of a gram.

Single-use issuance references and wallet-scoped redemption references prevent replay. Reserve quantities are role-reported, not independently verified gold.

02

The lifecycle

Hold tokens. Confirm settlement. Reconcile supply.

request → hold → approve → settle
Lifecycle details

Owners can cancel redemption before approval. An officer approves processing, then confirms settlement to burn tokens and reduce the recorded reserve.

Operational roles have separate permissions. Administrator handover uses a delayed, two-step process.

03

The interface

A local simulation. A separate testnet wallet.

integer units · explicit transitions

Development checks only. Not independently audited.

Verification details

Demo orders run on this device without blockchain transactions. The wallet connects separately to BSC Testnet for wallet-approved deployment.

The Solidity implementation is tested in a local EVM, including a 160-operation accounting model. The browser engine and interface are checked separately. These are development checks, not an independent security audit.
