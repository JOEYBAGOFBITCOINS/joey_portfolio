B7 / Protocol concept / November 18, 2026

Gold sets the reference.
The network carries the record.

A technical note on how the proposed ownership model would work.

Portfolio concept, not an investment offering. B7 has no live gold reserve, bank custody agreement, cash payout service, or physical-delivery service. The controls described as proposed are not implemented production features.

One denomination. Variable supply.

The proposed denomination is one B7 per gram of fine gold. There is no need to issue a million tokens at creation. The Solidity constructor starts with zero supply. New tokens would follow confirmed gold allocation, rather than precede it.

Outstanding B7 ≤ confirmed fine-gold backing in grams

The intended process is payment acceptance, gold purchase and allocation, custody confirmation, then token issuance. Orders remain pending until the required confirmations are complete; failures need a defined refund process. Existing inventory could support issuance only to the extent it is allocated, unencumbered, and not already backing outstanding tokens.

Reference value and transaction prices.

Gold reference value per B7 = gold price per troy ounce ÷ 31.1034768

LBMA defines one troy ounce as 31.1034768 grams. This reference is not a guaranteed exchange price. Purchase and redemption quotes would use executable prices, with spreads, fees and any currency conversion disclosed before acceptance.

Net cash redemption = redeemed grams × executable gold sale price per gram − disclosed charges

Growth means more customers, reserves and tokens represented. It does not change each token's proposed gram entitlement. Gold prices and a token's market price can fall.

Redeem without a mandatory BNB swap.

The proposed service would offer two distinct choices, subject to eligibility, availability, minimums and a confirmed quote:

  • Cash: sell the corresponding gold and pay the verified customer through a supported bank-payment route.
  • Physical gold: arrange an eligible bar or coin delivery, with fabrication, insurance, delivery costs and destination restrictions explained upfront.

Neither choice would require the customer to exchange B7 for BNB. BNB pays blockchain transaction fees, as described in BNB Chain's documentation. Token escrow, payout or delivery, token destruction and reserve release must be reconciled so the same claim cannot be settled twice. That design must also handle failed bank payments, cancellations and disputes before releasing escrow or retiring tokens. Exact timing and responsibilities require a production settlement design.

A price crash is different from a network outage.

A fall in BNB's market price would not by itself change the grams of gold allocated to B7. However, network disruption, impaired trading liquidity or loss of confidence could still affect access and market prices.

If the chain cannot reliably establish ownership or final settlement, affected issuance and redemption flows should pause. Independently maintained ownership and custody records would support investigation and reconciliation. Recovery or migration must prevent the original tokens and replacement claims from both remaining usable. A database backup alone cannot guarantee recovery or justify immediate payment.

These continuity, identity, inheritance and assisted-recovery services are proposed. The same protection and attentive support would apply across all membership levels.

What the prototype proves.

The demo Solidity contract limits issuance to the reserve quantity reported by an authorized reserve officer. It separates operational roles, rejects reused references, and models escrow and settlement. A reported reserve is not proof that physical gold exists; real custody evidence, reconciliation and independent verification are still required. The demo token grants no gold ownership or redemption entitlement. Those rights, including treatment if an operator or custodian fails, require separate legal and custody arrangements.

The browser account starts with fictional holdings and a fixed illustrative $100-per-gram quote so visitors can explore the workflow. Those sample balances are separate from the contract's zero initial supply and are not live gold prices or purchased assets.

This prototype uses BNB Smart Chain Testnet. It does not create a new blockchain or connect to live bullion purchasing, bank payouts or shipping.
